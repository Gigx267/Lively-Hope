Foundational Narrative

From One Divine Encounter
to a Lasting Legacy

In 1986, Rev. Benson Ezem had a life-altering encounter with Jesus Christ. What began as an intimate, personal commitment has grown structurally over decades into a dynamic engine touching thousands across Nigeria and global communities.

Alongside Rev. Mrs. Patricia Ezem, they have built unified expressions of the Gospel—raising global altars of praise, championing higher academic pathways, and advancing regional medical equity.
