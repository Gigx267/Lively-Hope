Channels of Transformation

Our Reach

For nearly four decades, LivelyHope World Outreach has cultivated ten distinct programme areas — each a channel of divine transformation touching lives through worship, education, healthcare, discipleship, and humanitarian care. These initiatives represent our holistic response to God's call, serving communities across Nigeria and beyond.

Flagship Programme

Sweet Fragrance

Est. 2004 Annual Event

The crown jewel of our worship ministry — Sweet Fragrance is an annual gathering that draws worshippers from across Nigeria. What began as a divine mandate to "raise an altar of praise" has blossomed into a powerful convention featuring choir competitions, spoken word performances, dramatic presentations, and the prestigious scholarship and music awards ceremony.

Running uninterrupted every year since 2004, each edition carries a unique theme — from "Godly Wisdom" (2025) to themes of restoration, faith, and divine presence. Sweet Fragrance has become a spiritual landmark, a place where lives are transformed through the power of worship and the Word.

Choir Competitions Spoken Word Drama Music Awards Scholarship Presentations

Divine Living Seminars

Est. 1993

Our longest-running teaching initiative, Divine Living Seminars bring life-transforming biblical teaching directly to communities, schools, and localities. These seminars address practical Christian living — from faith and family to purpose and destiny. Over three decades, thousands have encountered God's truth through these powerful, Spirit-led sessions that make the Word accessible and applicable to everyday life. The seminars remain a cornerstone of our outreach, equipping believers to live victoriously in every sphere of life.

Community Teaching School Outreach Faith & Family

Scholarship Programme

Est. 2005

Born from a divine prompting, our scholarship scheme has empowered over 40 graduates across medicine, law, engineering, pharmacy, nursing, architecture, and accountancy. A dedicated scholarship board oversees candidate selection through structured assessments. Awards are renewable until graduation, and many beneficiaries are now thriving professionals — doctors running clinics, engineers working internationally, and professionals across the UK and Nigeria making significant impact.

View Scholarship Details

Medical Outreach

Est. 2013

Taking healthcare to underserved communities across multiple Local Government Areas in Abia State and Port Harcourt. Our medical outreach programme provides free consultations, medications, health education, and referrals — demonstrating Christ's compassion through practical care. Teams of volunteer medical professionals serve hundreds of patients during each outreach, addressing both physical and spiritual needs with dignity and love.

Free Consultations Medications Health Education

Thirsty Souls

Est. 2007

A dedicated women's discipleship and spiritual formation programme that creates sacred spaces for women to encounter God, grow in faith, and discover their divine purpose. Through regular gatherings, retreats, mentoring relationships, and Bible study, Thirsty Souls equips women to thrive spiritually and become agents of transformation in their families, churches, and communities.

Retreats Mentoring Bible Study

YWAM Partnership

Est. 2010

In strategic partnership with Youth With A Mission (YWAM) and the University of the Nations, we offer discipleship training and leadership development programmes that prepare young people for global mission. This collaboration combines YWAM's proven training model with our local contextual understanding, equipping a new generation of leaders for Kingdom impact across nations and cultures.

Discipleship Leadership Training Global Mission

LivelyHope Church

Est. 2010

The church ministry arm of our outreach, with headquarters in Port Harcourt and a growing branch in Abuja. LivelyHope Church serves as a spiritual home for believers — a centre for dynamic worship, sound biblical teaching, and authentic community. It is the gathering point from which many of our outreach initiatives flow, nurturing disciples who go out to impact their world with the living hope of Jesus Christ.

Port Harcourt HQ Abuja Branch

Out of the Mouth of Babes

Ongoing

Our vibrant children's ministry that nurtures faith from the earliest years. Through the annual Easter conference, weekly Bible clubs in communities, and the Little Blessings initiative, children discover God's love in engaging, age-appropriate ways. We believe in raising a generation that knows their God and walks in His purposes from childhood — truly, "out of the mouth of babes" God ordains praise and establishes His kingdom.

Easter Conference Bible Clubs Little Blessings

Love Basket

Ongoing

Practical compassion in action — Love Basket provides food packages, essential supplies, and humanitarian support to the less privileged, crisis victims, and vulnerable families across our communities. Whether responding to emergencies or supporting those in chronic need, this programme demonstrates God's love in tangible, measurable ways, ensuring that no one in our reach goes hungry or feels forgotten.

Food Baskets Crisis Support Essential Supplies

Hope Publications

Ongoing

Spreading truth through the anointed written word. Hope Publications produces and distributes spiritual resources including The Secret of Growth, The Lord My Healer, Walking by Faith, Godly Wisdom, and other life-changing materials. These publications extend our teaching reach beyond events and gatherings, placing timeless biblical wisdom into hands and homes across the nation, transforming lives one page at a time.
