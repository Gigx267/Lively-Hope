Channels of Transformation

Our Reach

For nearly four decades, LivelyHope World Outreach has cultivated ten distinct programme areas — each a channel of divine transformation touching lives through worship, education, healthcare, discipleship, and humanitarian care.

Flagship Programme

Sweet Fragrance

Est. 2004 Annual Event

The crown jewel of our worship ministry — Sweet Fragrance is an annual gathering that draws worshippers from across Nigeria. What began as a divine mandate to "raise an altar of praise" has blossomed into a powerful convention featuring choir competitions, spoken word performances, dramatic presentations, and scholarship awards.

Choir Competitions Spoken Word Music Awards

Divine Living Seminars

Est. 1993

Our longest-running teaching initiative, Divine Living Seminars bring life-transforming biblical teaching directly to communities, schools, and localities — equipping believers to live victoriously.

Scholarship Programme

Est. 2005

Empowered over 40 graduates across medicine, law, engineering, pharmacy, nursing, architecture, and accountancy. Renewable annually until graduation.

Medical Outreach

Est. 2013

Free consultations, medications, health education across Abia State and Port Harcourt — demonstrating Christ's compassion through practical care.

Thirsty Souls

Est. 2007

Women's discipleship and spiritual formation programme — retreats, mentoring, and Bible study equipping women to thrive spiritually.

YWAM Partnership

Est. 2010

Strategic partnership with Youth With A Mission offering discipleship training and leadership development for global mission.

LivelyHope Church

Est. 2010

Spiritual home with headquarters in Port Harcourt and a growing branch in Abuja — dynamic worship, biblical teaching, authentic community.

Out of the Mouth of Babes

Ongoing

Children's ministry nurturing faith from the earliest years — Easter conference, Bible clubs, and Little Blessings initiative.

Love Basket

Ongoing

Food packages, essential supplies, and humanitarian support to the less privileged, crisis victims, and vulnerable families.

Hope Publications

Ongoing
