Chronicle of Grace

Ministry Timeline

Four decades of faithful service and Kingdom impact — tracing God's faithfulness from 1986 to today.

1986

Founder Rev. Benson Ezem encounters Christ. Spiritual journey begins.

1988

Marries Patricia Ezem. Partnership in life and ministry formed.

1990

Receives divine call to Gospel outreach.

1991–92

Relocates from Kano to Port Harcourt following civil unrest.

1993–96

Divine Living Seminars launched across communities and schools in Abia State.

1998

Commissioned to plant a new church. Serves as senior pastor.

1999

Outreach to Umuahia — school supplies, food, and humanitarian support distributed.

2004

Sweet Fragrance worship programme founded. LHWOF formally established.

2005

Scholarship scheme launched. Ministry legally incorporated (15 September 2005).

2007

Thirsty Souls women's discipleship programme established.

2010

YWAM partnership formed. LivelyHope Church established.

2013

Medical outreach programme begins across Abia State and Port Harcourt.

2025 & Beyond

Sweet Fragrance theme: 'Godly Wisdom'. Ministry continues to grow and expand globally.
