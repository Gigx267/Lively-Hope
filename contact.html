Quick Information

Frequently Asked

How can I partner with the ministry?

Visit our Partner With Us page to explore giving options, or contact our partnership team directly at partnerships@livelyhope.org.

How do I apply for a scholarship?

Scholarship applications are managed by our scholarship board. Contact us with the subject "Scholarship Inquiry" and we'll guide you through the process.

When is Sweet Fragrance held?

Sweet Fragrance is an annual worship gathering. Dates and themes are announced each year. Follow our social media or contact us for the latest information.

Where is LivelyHope Church located?

We have locations in Port Harcourt (headquarters) and Abuja (branch). Our community base is in Ubakala, Abia State.
