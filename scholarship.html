Divine Mandate for Education

Raising Professionals
Through Kingdom Investment

Launched in 2005 following a divine prompting, the scholarship scheme has supported over 40 graduates across medicine, law, engineering, healthcare, finance, and more.

Many beneficiaries are now thriving professionals — doctors running clinics, engineers working internationally, professionals across the UK and Nigeria — each a living testament to the transformative power of Kingdom investment in education.

A dedicated scholarship board oversees candidate selection through structured assessments. Awards are granted per academic session and renewed until graduation, ensuring sustained support throughout each student's academic journey.
